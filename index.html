02 / About

Who is
VoidWatcher?

VoidWatcher is a crypto-native creator focused on the intersection of Web3, internet culture and attention.

I create content around crypto, NFTs, memecoins, trading, emerging projects and the narratives forming around them.

Some posts are analytical. Some are educational. Some are completely unserious.

That's intentional. The goal is simple: make content that people actually want to stop and read.
